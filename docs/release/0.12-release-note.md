# Version 0.12: what changed, in plain words

Status: DRAFT for Waleed's check. Version number is still 0.11.0 in the app until he says OK (then 0.12.0). Written 17 Nov 2026 by the helper session. I cannot see the bug tracker, so **each line should be matched to its B-number by the manager before this is published**; nothing below goes beyond what the handoff notes say.

## For schools
**Pictures that used to go blank now show themselves.**
- The face in "Is this ...?" no longer comes up empty after Clean up or while the app is busy: pictures someone is waiting for go first.
- After you move photos to the Bin, empty the Bin and import different photos, new photos open as themselves, not as an old picture.
- A person's main-photo circle keeps their face even if you edit that photo in another app.
- Books: iPhone (HEIC) photos and RAW-only photos now print in the PDF.
- Albums: no event card is left empty.

**Faces:** suggestions still wait for a person's yes. Two small extra face models (eyes and smiles) are inside the app but no screen uses them yet.

**Privacy:** photos of a student who "can't appear" are re-checked when you change that from a class list or press Undo, and the website copies are updated too.

**Speed:** the website opens faster and the Library is quicker with large imports.

**Windows:** fixes for the Windows app (folders, archive drive).

**Security:** a round of fixes on the website, release files and packaging.

**Drive unplugged while scanning:** photos are no longer marked "missing" if the library drive drops out half way through a check. A single failing job can no longer freeze the app's picture queue.

## One thing to know (a trade-off)
To make the website faster, sign-in is now checked from the sign-in token (Supabase's own advice). **If you remove a person from your school, they can keep web access until their token runs out, at most about 1 hour.** Nothing else changes.

## Not in 0.12
Captions with a student's name (warn, not block) and the Gemini retry time: these are on the captions branch and come later. The owner page, plans and licences are release 0.13. The encrypted Mac database is 0.14.

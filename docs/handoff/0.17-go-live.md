## What changed
- Meta busy (rate limit, "please retry", timeout): the post goes back in line and is tried again after 5, 15 and 45 minutes by itself; the post page says when. Only after the 4th try does it fail.
- A real problem fails at once with a plain Arabic or English reason (photo refused, text refused, Instagram's daily limit, same text just posted, not connected, changed after approval), with Meta's own words underneath.
- Facebook signed the school out (password changed, app removed, permission taken away): Accounts shows "Reconnect Facebook" in red with how many posts are waiting, the accounts show "Signed out", the team gets one alert (Slack/Teams now; email once L-40 is done), and nothing more is sent to Meta until someone reconnects or Check connection says the key works again.
- Never posted twice: if Facebook or Instagram made the post but the answer got lost, the next try finds that post instead of posting again (Instagram by the saved container, Facebook by the same text on the Page).
- Instagram rules checked again just before posting (photo shape, caption 2,200 characters, 30 hashtags, 20 photos, 20 mentions); the post screen now also blocks an Instagram caption over 2,200 characters. "Fix shape" (already there) makes 4:5 copies.
- Published posts show "View on Facebook" and "View on Instagram".
- Accounts > Check connection: asks Meta what the connection may do and lists posting, Instagram, comments, results and private messages as Works / Needs Reconnect. This is the private-messages Reconnect check from 0.15.
- Reconnect keeps the Page the school chose before (it used to pick the first Page with Instagram).
- Owner > Keys and switches: every free key, On or Off, the exact Vercel name to add, where to get it, Test buttons for Gemini and Meta. Values are never shown.
- Google and Microsoft sign-in buttons show by themselves once switched on in Supabase (Authentication > Sign In / Providers). AUTH_GOOGLE / AUTH_MICROSOFT are no longer needed ("off" still hides a button).
